# Profile Repository Guidelines

This repository's `README.md` is displayed publicly on GitHub.

## Privacy & Repository References
- **Do not include names or links to other repositories** (e.g., from sibling directories or local workspaces) unless the user explicitly names them or confirms they are public. Most local repositories may be private.
- When drawing context from other repositories, **synthesize at a general level**: describe engineering domains, architectures, technologies, and capabilities rather than specific project titles or URLs.
